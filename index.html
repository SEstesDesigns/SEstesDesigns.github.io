About this portfolio

What you're looking at

A program with three connected courses:

  • Assess Your Strengths: An interactive self-assessment I built with an AI coding partner using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Community members rate themselves across six dimensions, indicate their preferences, and receive role recommendations. The output includes a team dashboard and an exportable development plan populated by the learner.
  • Before You Build: A course design document I built for a five-session virtual ILT program. It demonstrates curriculum architecture, learning objective development, activity design, and a structured practitioner engagement model.
  • Who Fits Where? A branching eLearning scenario I built in Articulate Storyline using ElevenLabs cloned voiceover. Individuals and group leaders practice deciding what role assignments work based on strengths and preferences with consequence-based feedback. No right answers, just tradeoffs that build judgment. Segment 1 is fully built; reference to segments 2 and 3 indicate what's next.

Tools that ignore existing expertise produce teams that duplicate effort.
